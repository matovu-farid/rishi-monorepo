First release. Rishi reads with you and talks back.

Library
- Drop EPUB or PDF files into Rishi from Files, AirDrop, or any share sheet.
- Fast grid with covers, progress, and a Reading Now shelf.
- Locale-aware search across titles, authors, and metadata.
- Sample book bundled on first run.

PDF Reader
- PDFKit-powered viewer with single-page and continuous modes.
- Highlights with notes in four colors, persisted per-book.
- Table of contents sheet, outline jumps, and page thumbnails.
- Light, dark, and sepia backdrops.

EPUB Reader
- Readium-powered reflowable + fixed-layout EPUB support.
- Adjustable font size, font family, line spacing, and margins.
- Highlights with notes via CFI selection.
- Table of contents and search.

Sync
- Cross-device position, highlights, and notes via silent push.
- Background sync windows on iOS, scene-restored sessions on Mac.
- Manual pull-to-refresh on every screen.

Read Aloud
- High-quality narration with lock-screen and AirPods controls.
- Background playback, configurable voice, and per-book resume.
- Sentence-level highlighting tracks the spoken text.

Chat
- Ask questions about the book you're reading.
- Conversations grouped per-book, with full history.
- Streamed answers grounded in the current chapter or selection.

Voice Chat (Rishi Pro)
- Real-time spoken conversation about your reading.
- Push-to-talk and continuous modes.
- Microphone permission primer explains why access is needed.

Mac Catalyst
- Real sidebar with library + conversations.
- Full keyboard shortcuts, menu bar commands, and scene restoration.
- Universal Links open books straight from Safari, Mail, or Messages.

Accessibility
- VoiceOver labels across the reader, library, and chat.
- Full Dynamic Type support up to AX5.
- AA contrast verified on every surface.
- Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast respected.

Account
- Sign in with Apple — private relay email supported.
- One-tap account deletion in Settings.
- Anonymous diagnostics can be turned off.

Subscription
- Rishi Pro subscriptions are managed at rishi.fidexa.org.
- Open the page from Settings to subscribe, change, or cancel.
