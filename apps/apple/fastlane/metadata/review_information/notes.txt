Thanks for reviewing Rishi.

SIGN-IN FLOW
- Sign in with Apple is the only test path. No shared credentials are needed; the reviewer's Apple ID is sufficient.
- Google Sign-In is also available but Apple is the recommended review path.
- Account deletion is available in Settings → Account → Delete Account. This calls our worker which performs Apple /auth/revoke server-side, then deletes the user row.

FIRST-RUN CONTENT
- A sample EPUB ("The Sample Book") ships in the bundle and is auto-installed into the library on first launch. The reviewer does not need to import their own content to exercise the reader, highlights, chat, or read-aloud surfaces.

VOICE CHAT (PRO FEATURE)
- Real-time voice chat is gated on an Apple subscription. The submitted Release/TestFlight build uses native StoreKit purchase and entitlement sync.
- To test microphone access, start the voice conversation flow. Rishi first explains that microphone audio is used only during an active conversation, then tap Continue on the in-app permission screen and allow the system prompt.

UNIVERSAL LINKS
- Universal Links to https://rishi.fidexa.org/book/<id> are wired in the app but the worker-side AASA endpoint is not yet active in production. Deep links via rishi:// custom scheme still work from the simulator.

BILLING
- Rishi uses native StoreKit in-app purchases. From a premium feature, the reviewer can open the paywall, choose a Reader or Voice plan, and complete the Apple purchase flow. The paywall also provides Restore Purchases; Settings opens Apple's in-app subscription management sheet.
- For a real purchase test, use the `rishi (Sandbox)` scheme on a device with an App Store Connect Sandbox tester, or install the uploaded build through TestFlight and use the Sandbox Apple Account. The app's Apple sign-in account is separate from the Sandbox purchase account.

PRIVACY
- See PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy in the bundle and the App Privacy section of this submission. No tracking SDKs are bundled. Sentry crash data is first-party and can be disabled in Settings → Diagnostics.
