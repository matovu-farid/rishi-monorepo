Proposed Rishi pitch-deck story

10 slides • calm reading intelligence • evidence-based draft

1

The promise

Make every page more understandable. Rishi turns reading into an active, conversational experience.

2

The problem

Important knowledge is trapped in long, dense documents—hard to read, search, remember, and discuss.

3

The product

Import books and documents; read them in a focused reader; listen aloud; search text and meaning; ask questions in context.

4

Voice changes the interface

Rishi supports read-aloud and voice conversation so users can learn while walking, commuting, or working.

5

The AI layer

Document text, vector search, retrieval, and book-specific conversations connect AI answers to what the user is reading.

6

One continuous workflow

Library → reader → highlights/bookmarks → search → conversation, with sync across devices.

7

Who it is for

Students, researchers, professionals, and lifelong learners who need to understand more in less time. Market sizing is a placeholder.

8

Proof and traction

Prototype and shipped capabilities are documented; user, usage, revenue, and validation metrics need your input.

9

Why Rishi / why now

Founder story, African builder perspective, and the opportunity created by multimodal and conversational AI.

10

The ask

What support from Google AI Lab would unlock: product acceleration, model expertise, distribution, compute, or partnership.
