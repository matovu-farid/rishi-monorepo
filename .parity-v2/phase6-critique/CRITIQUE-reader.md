# Reader UX Critique — Rishi Mobile (Phase 6)

## Summary
EPUB reader is feature-complete vs electron; PDF, MOBI, and DJVU readers are
*sheets={{}}*-shaped shells with no TOC, bookmarks, highlights, search, or
appearance sheets despite ReaderShell supporting them and the desktop having
all of them. Toolbar auto-hide is non-resilient (any tap inside the bottom bar
re-fires the 3s timer from scratch and the user has no way to pin it open),
and several reader-screen Pressables (full-screen tap-toggles, MOBI/DJVU
nav arrows, AnnotationPopover swatches) ship below Apple HIG's 44pt minimum.

## Notes
- Touch-target audits assume 1× scale at default iOS dynamic-type size; users on Larger Accessibility Sizes will widen text but not buttons, exacerbating RDR-009/010/011.
- The reader auto-hide timer is 3s (ReaderShell.tsx:122). Apple Books uses 5s and Books.app also keeps the bottom bar visible while the user is highlighting — RDR-006 covers the parity-by-fallback ("pin") path.
- ReaderShellContext exposes `bottomBarVisible` + `toggleToolbar`, but no method to *show* without flipping — extending the context with `showToolbar(reason: 'user' | 'selection' | 'tts')` would also let read-aloud, voice chat, and selection paths force-show the chrome (a P2 follow-on to RDR-006).
