# Phase 6 — Sign-in & Premium Gating UX Critique

## Summary
The premium gate sheet renders cleanly and shares copy with the rest of the suite, but the post-sign-in resume contract is broken on chat (typed message is silently dropped when the gate is dismissed), the sheet inflicts a hard-coded Apple/Google split that diverges from worker capabilities and the email path on the sign-in screen, and `useRequireAuth` will optimistically fire premium actions before auth is hydrated. There is no escape hatch from the sign-in screen, no sign-up affordance, and signed-out users have no upfront "Pro" hint anywhere in the chrome — discovery of paywalls is entirely reactive. Several lower-severity polish defects (focus restore on close, error copy granularity, dead `shouldGate` export) round out the list.
