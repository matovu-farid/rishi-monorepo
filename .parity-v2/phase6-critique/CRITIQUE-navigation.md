# CRITIQUE — Navigation & Flow

## Summary
EPUB/MOBI/DJVU readers boot with the top bar hidden, hiding the only on-screen "Back to library" affordance — the user-reported dead-end. Tap-to-reveal regions for MOBI/DJVU only cover the center 24% of the page, so users tapping near the edges get no response. Several secondary paths (chat source-link, deep-link cold start, sign-in screen, conversations list) assume a navigation stack that may not exist or routes to the wrong reader format.
