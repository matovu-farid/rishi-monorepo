Rishi turns the books on your devices into conversations. Read your EPUB and PDF library on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, then ask questions, get summaries, or talk through a passage out loud — all without leaving the page.

Bring your own books. Drop EPUB or PDF files into Rishi and they live in a fast, searchable library with covers, progress, and a Reading Now shelf. Highlights and notes follow you across devices.

Read the way you like. A focused, customizable reader handles fixed-layout and reflowable EPUBs, scanned PDFs with a real text layer, and table-of-contents jumps. Pick light, dark, or sepia. Adjust font size and spacing. Search every page.

Pick up where you left off. Open a book on your Mac, keep reading on iPhone on the train, finish on iPad in bed — Rishi syncs your position, highlights, and notes in the background using silent push.

Chat with any book. Ask "what does this chapter argue?" or "summarize this scene," and Rishi answers grounded in the book you're reading. Conversations are saved per-book and per-session, so you can come back to the thread later.

Talk it through. Real-time voice chat lets you have a natural spoken conversation about what you're reading — ideal for studying, language practice, or thinking out loud on a walk. (Voice chat requires Rishi Pro.)

Read aloud. High-quality narration plays through any speaker or AirPods, with background playback, lock-screen controls, and a configurable voice. Listen while you commute, cook, or rest your eyes.

Made for Apple silicon. Optimized for Mac Catalyst with a real sidebar, full keyboard navigation, native menus, and scene restoration. Universal Links open books straight from Safari, Mail, or Messages.

Accessible by default. VoiceOver labels across the reader, library, and chat. Full Dynamic Type support. AA contrast on every surface. Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast respected.

Private by design. Sign in with Apple — no password to manage, no email required. Your reading data lives in your account, not in an ad network. Anonymous diagnostics can be turned off in Settings.

Pro features (subscription). Voice chat, unlimited chat conversations, and premium narration voices are part of Rishi Pro. Subscriptions are managed at rishi.fidexa.org — open the page from Settings to subscribe, change, or cancel.
