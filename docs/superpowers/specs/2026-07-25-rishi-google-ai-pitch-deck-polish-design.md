# Rishi Google AI Lab Pitch Deck — Polish Design

## Objective

Refine the existing Google Slides deck into a polished, application-ready product brief without changing its factual stage framing or story arc.

## Approved visual direction: Bright product brief

- Warm paper canvas as the primary background.
- Deep navy for primary text and major dark panels.
- Cobalt/blue rules and small labels for navigation and emphasis.
- Strong editorial hierarchy: compact uppercase kicker, large concise headline, short supporting copy.
- Use rounded cards and restrained borders for technical/product details.
- Keep the Product Demo slide visually richer and darker so the embedded video remains the focal proof point.
- Keep Apple screenshots large, aligned, and visually consistent; avoid decorative clutter.

## Content and layout changes

- Tighten slide copy so each slide communicates one idea quickly.
- Use a consistent two-column rhythm: narrative on the left, product evidence or structured cards on the right.
- Convert technical capability lists into benefit-led cards where possible.
- Preserve the existing Apple-first, launch-stage, pre-revenue framing.
- Keep the embedded demo video on the Product Demo slide.
- Keep explicit placeholders for App Store status/date and founder details; do not invent metrics or market claims.

## Success criteria

- The deck reads as one coherent product brief rather than a sequence of text-heavy slides.
- Text remains legible at normal presentation and thumbnail sizes.
- Screenshots, logo, and video feel intentionally placed rather than appended.
- No unsupported claims, fabricated traction, revenue, or market data are introduced.
- Google Slides remains editable and saved to Drive.

- Implementation should be verified by reviewing the cover, product/demo, screenshot, measurement, and ask slides in the browser.
